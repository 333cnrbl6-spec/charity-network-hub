# CharityHub User Onboarding Guide

Welcome to CharityHub! This guide will help you set up your account and get productive in 15 minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Account (2 minutes)

1. Go to https://charityhub.com/landing
2. Click "Start Free Trial"
3. Enter:
   - Organization name (e.g., "ACME Charity")
   - Your email
   - Password
4. Click "Create Account"

✅ You now have a 30-day free trial with all features unlocked.

---

## Step 2: Set Up Your Charity Profile (3 minutes)

1. Click "Settings" → "Organization Profile"
2. Fill in:
   - **Charity name**
   - **Charity number** (optional but recommended)
   - **Cause area** (Health, Education, Poverty, etc.)
   - **Location**
   - **Contact email**
3. Upload your logo (optional)
4. Click "Save"

✅ Your profile is now complete.

---

## Step 3: Invite Your Team (5 minutes)

1. Click "Settings" → "Team Members"
2. Click "Invite New Member"
3. Enter email addresses (comma-separated for multiple)
4. Select role:
   - **Admin:** Full access + can invite others
   - **Editor:** Can create/edit data + reports
   - **Viewer:** Read-only access
5. Click "Send Invitations"

✅ Team members will receive email invites.

---

## Step 4: Import Your Donor Data (3 minutes)

1. Click "Data" → "Import"
2. Choose upload method:
   - **CSV file:** Upload spreadsheet of donors
   - **Paste JSON:** Paste structured data
   - **Manual entry:** Add one-by-one
3. Map columns:
   - Name → Donor Name
   - Email → Donor Email
   - Phone → Phone (optional)
   - Amount → Total Donated (optional)
4. Click "Import"

✅ Donors are now in your database.

---

## Step 5: Create Your First Campaign (2 minutes)

1. Click "Campaigns" → "New Campaign"
2. Enter:
   - **Campaign name** (e.g., "Annual Appeal 2026")
   - **Goal amount** (e.g., £5,000)
   - **End date** (e.g., 31 Dec 2026)
   - **Description** (optional)
3. Click "Create Campaign"

✅ Campaign created.

## Key Features Tour

### Donors
**Location:** Menu → Donors

Track all donor information:
- Name, email, phone
- Donation history
- Last donation date
- Total donated
- Preferred contact method

**Actions:**
- Add new donor
- Edit donor details
- Send email
- Log donation

### Campaigns
**Location:** Menu → Campaigns

Manage fundraising campaigns:
- Set fundraising goal
- Track progress in real-time
- Assign team members
- Email campaign details to donors

**Actions:**
- Create campaign
- View progress
- Export to PDF
- Share campaign link

### Reports
**Location:** Menu → Reports

Built-in reports:
- **Donor Summary:** Total donors, avg donation, retention rate
- **Campaign Performance:** How close to goal, top donors
- **Fundraising Trend:** Donations over time
- **Email Performance:** Open rates, click rates

### Compliance
**Location:** Menu → Compliance

Stay compliant with:
- Gift Aid eligibility tracker
- GDPR consent management
- Annual return checklists
- Training expiry alerts

## Common Tasks

### How do I track a donation?

1. Click "Donors"
2. Find donor (use search)
3. Click donor name
4. Scroll to "Donations"
5. Click "Log Donation"
6. Enter amount, date, campaign
7. Click "Save"

### How do I send an email to a group?

1. Click "Donors"
2. Filter to group (e.g., "Donated in last year")
3. Click "Select All"
4. Click "Send Email"
5. Write message (or use template)
6. Click "Send"

### How do I export data?

1. Click "Reports"
2. Select report type
3. Choose date range
4. Click "Export as CSV" or "Export as PDF"

### How do I change my plan?

1. Click "Settings" → "Billing"
2. Current plan shown at top
3. Click "Upgrade" to see options
4. Choose new plan
5. Payment will be adjusted (pro-rata if upgrading mid-month)

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't log in"
- Check email for typos
- Use "Forgot password" to reset
- Contact support: support@charityhub.com

### "My import failed"
- Check file format (CSV or JSON)
- Verify column names match expected fields
- Try importing smaller batch first
- Check our CSV template in Help Center

### "I don't see my data"
- Refresh page (Cmd+R or Ctrl+R)
- Check you're logged into correct organization
- Verify data was imported (check "Data" → "Import History")

### "Team member can't access"
- Check they accepted email invitation
- Verify role has correct permissions
- Have them try logging out and back in
