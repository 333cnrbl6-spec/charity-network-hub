# CharityHub Sales Pitch - May 2026

## Executive Summary
CharityHub is an enterprise-grade volunteer and charity management platform that automates compliance, scales volunteer impact, and gives donors real-time community outcomes. Built for Age UK and multi-branch charities managing 50-10,000+ volunteers.

**Traditional Charity Operations:**
- ❌ Volunteers scattered across spreadsheets & WhatsApp groups

- ❌ Safeguarding incidents tracked inconsistently
- ❌ Grant writing takes weeks (multiple staff hours)
- ❌ No real-time visibility into community impact

**CharityHub Solution:**
- ✅ Centralized volunteer database with auto-matching
- ✅ AI assigns best volunteer to each job in seconds

- ✅ AI generates grant applications in minutes
- ✅ Public impact dashboard for donors (shareable, exportable)

## Key Features (Pitch Talking Points)

### 1. Smart Volunteer Matching
"In seconds, our AI analyzes 100+ volunteers and recommends the top 5 best matches based on skills, location, and availability. Reduce admin time by 60%."
- Skills-based matching (40 points)
- Location proximity scoring (30 points)
- Availability matching (30 points)
- One-click assignment with auto-email to volunteer

**Use Case:** A handyman job in Manchester gets matched to 3 local volunteers with home maintenance skills available that day. Assigned in 10 seconds instead of 2 hours of admin work.

### 2. Automated Safeguarding
"Never miss a DBS renewal or safeguarding follow-up again. Automated reminders at 30, 14, and 7 days before expiry, plus 48-hour incident follow-up checks."
- Daily DBS expiry monitoring
- Email + in-app alerts (multi-channel)
- 48-hour incident follow-up automation (runs every 6 hours)
- Complete incident dashboard with severity tracking
- Audit trail for compliance

**Use Case:** A volunteer's DBS expires in 30 days → Automated email sent to volunteer + safeguarding lead. At 7 days, escalated to high-priority in-app alert. At 48-hour mark post-incident, safeguarding lead automatically reminded to document follow-up assessment.

### 3. Real-Time Impact Analytics
"Show donors exactly where their money goes. Public shareable dashboard with live metrics, visual charts, and PDF exports."
- **4 Key Metrics:** Hours contributed, beneficiaries supported, active volunteers, grant funding
- **4 Charts:** Volunteer trends, activity overview, beneficiary outcomes, grant sources
- **Exportable:** Professional PDF report for grant applications & donor presentations
- **Public Shareable:** Share URL with donors, no login required

**Use Case:** Donor asks "How many people did my donation help?" Share `/impact` dashboard. They see 500+ beneficiaries, 2,000 volunteer hours, £150k granted. Generates PDF report to include in next funding pitch.

### 4. AI-Powered Grant Writing
"What took 2 weeks of research and writing now takes 15 minutes. Auto-generate grant applications, impact reports, and thank-you letters."
- Grant application sections auto-generated (executive summary, outcomes, budget justification)
- Context-aware using real data (hours, beneficiaries, outcomes)
- One-click PDF export
- Editable drafts for customization

**Use Case:** Grant deadline in 3 days. Instead of panic, coordinator inputs brief project details. AI generates complete application. Team edits for 30 mins. Submitted before deadline.

### 5. Multi-Branch Coordination
"Manage 5, 10, or 50 branches from one dashboard. Real-time data sync, network-wide insights, and cross-branch resource coordination."
- National dashboard with branch status
- Real-time sync across regions
- Regional performance reports
- Coordinator portals per branch
- Network-wide volunteer pool

**Use Case:** Age UK Bury coordinator can see Manchester has 2 handyperson volunteers but 5 jobs. Borrow 1 volunteer from nearby Stockport. System tracks cross-branch assignments.
