# CharityHub Customer Success Guide

Our goal: **Your charity raises more money and wastes less time.**

We succeed when you see measurable results in the first 30 days:
- ✅ All donor data imported
- ✅ Team invited and using platform
- ✅ First campaign tracked
- ✅ Time saved quantified

## Common Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge: "We have years of donor data in spreadsheets"
**Solution:**
- Provide CSV import template
- Clean data for them (up to 1,000 rows free)
- Offer step-by-step migration plan
- Estimate: 2-4 hours work

### Challenge: "Team won't use the platform"
**Solution:**
- Identify power user (internal champion)
- Have them train the rest
- Start with critical function only (donations)
- Celebrate early wins

### Challenge: "We don't see immediate ROI"
**Solution:**
- Measure time saved, not just dollars
- Set up automated reports for transparency
- Compare donation volume month-over-month
- Often see 30% increase in 90 days

### Challenge: "We're on trial and thinking about canceling"
**Solution:**
- Offer 14-day extension (full data import time)
- Share success stories from similar charities
- Discuss custom pricing for nonprofit (Pro tier free for <5 staff)
- Emphasize zero-risk: cancel anytime

## Customer Health Score

We monitor 5 indicators:

| Indicator | Healthy | At Risk | Churning |
|-----------|---------|---------|----------|
| **Login frequency** | 5+ per week | 1-2 per week | None in 30d |
| **Data currency** | Updated weekly | Updated monthly | Stale (60d+) |
| **Feature usage** | 5+ features | 2-3 features | Only 1 feature |
| **Team adoption** | 3+ users | 1-2 users | 1 user only |
| **Support tickets** | Few, resolved | Some, slow | Many, unresolved |

If score drops below "healthy," we proactively reach out with:
- Free troubleshooting call
- Feature training
- Content customization
- Discount/incentive to stay
