# CharityHub Sales Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: Problem Statement

**"Charities waste 15+ hours per week managing donors, campaigns, and compliance manually."**

Current state:
- Spreadsheets scattered across email and drives
- Manual data entry from forms, emails, phone calls
- No real-time visibility into donor trends
- Compliance tracking is fragmented and error-prone
- Team members work in silos with no collaboration

---

## Slide 2: Solution

**CharityHub: One platform to manage everything**

- 📊 Centralized donor database (400+ data points)
- 💰 Campaign management with real-time tracking
- 📋 Automated compliance checklists
- 📧 Email automation & donor journey tracking
- 🤖 AI-powered grant writing assistant
- 📱 Mobile app for field work

**Result:** Save 10+ hours/week, increase donations by 30%, never miss compliance deadlines

## Key Talking Points

### "Why charities?"
- High switching costs (5+ years of data)
- Recurring revenue (sticky business)
- Mission-driven = loyal customers
- Less price-sensitive than SMB market

### "What's your competitive advantage?"
- Built FOR charities (not adapted from enterprise tools)
- 60% cheaper than enterprise solutions
- No long-term contracts (removes friction)
- Free for teams under 5 people

### "How do you beat larger competitors?"
- Speed: We iterate weekly vs quarterly
- Focus: Charities only (deep expertise)
- Community: Transparent roadmap, user-driven
- Price: 70% cheaper than Salesforce for nonprofits

### "What's the biggest risk?"
- **Risk:** Slow customer acquisition (SMB CAC is 12-18 weeks)
- **Mitigation:** Early partnerships with charity networks (direct access to 10k+ orgs)
